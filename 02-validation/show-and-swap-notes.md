# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

https://mdeleon86.github.io/vibe-coding-project/02-validation/

Signal V3 compares a traditional 12-chart dashboard against an insight-first dashboard to test whether users can identify the most important issue and recommended action faster, with less abandonment.

## What my partner understood (in their words)

The hypothesis is that users abandon dashboards because important insights are difficult to find. Highlighting the key insight and recommended action should improve understanding and reduce abandonment. The kill switch is triggered if the redesigned dashboard's bounce rate remains at or above 60%.

## Where they got confused / pushed back

The comparison may not be entirely fair because Version B displays the important insight immediately, while Version A requires users to search for it. Version A also takes fewer clicks than the six-click baseline. The synthetic data makes the prototype useful for testing, but it does not establish how real users would behave.

## What I'll change as a result

Make Version A more representative of the original six-click dashboard experience so the comparison with Version B is more credible. Keep the same underlying data and test task for both versions.

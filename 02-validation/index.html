Signal V3 · Hypothesis test

Does an insight-first landing view beat the 12-chart dashboard?

We believe replacing the 12-chart landing view with a dashboard highlighting the most important metric, a plain-language explanation, and a recommended action will cause users to identify actionable insights faster and be less likely to abandon the dashboard, for B2B SaaS product managers and growth leads who use the dashboard to make decisions.

We'll know we're right when

  1. The dashboard bounce rate drops from 60% to below 40%.
  2. At least 70% of test users identify the key insight and recommended action within 30 seconds.

Kill switch

If the dashboard bounce rate remains at or above 60% after introducing a guided headline and recommended action, investigate whether we're highlighting the wrong metric and pivot if necessary.

How the test works

  1. Each participant gets the same task and is assigned one version: A, today's 12-chart view, or B, the insight-first view. Both show the same synthetic data.
  2. They press I found it when they know the key insight and what to do, or I'd close this dashboard if they would normally give up.
  3. They then pick the insight and the action from short lists.
  4. Results compare A and B against the success criteria and the kill switch.
What this prototype measures: time to "I found it", whether the insight and action were correct, explicit abandonment, time to first interaction, and clicks.
What it can't measure: the real bounce rate, which needs live traffic. Explicit abandonment is used as the stand-in for bounce. No interaction in the first 15 seconds is tracked separately and is never counted as abandonment.
Facilitator: set the version, then hand the device to the participant.

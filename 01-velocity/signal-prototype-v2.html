What do these metrics mean?
Churn rate
The percentage of existing customer accounts that cancel during a given period.
Activation rate
The percentage of new users or accounts that complete a defined first-value action.
Trial-to-paid conversion
The percentage of trial accounts that become paying customers.
New bookings
The value of newly contracted business during the period.
How are health ratings decided?
  • Healthy Green: within the expected range.
  • Caution Amber: near or slightly outside the expected range. Worth monitoring.
  • Needs attention Red: well outside the expected range. Act on it.

Thresholds are illustrative, based on synthetic product assumptions, not verified industry benchmarks. Ratings use the value as shown on the dashboard.

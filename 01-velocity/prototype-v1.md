# Prototype V1: Signal

**Scenario:** 04, The Dashboard Nobody Reads
**Launch path:** A, Copy & Customize
**Builder:** Claude Code (local); plain HTML/CSS in `01-velocity/v1/`
**Data:** synthetic, hand-written. No real customer or company data.

## Prompt used
Official Launch Path A starter prompt, unchanged:
> Build me an analytics dashboard that surfaces the single most important insight first. Include one headline metric with a plain-language takeaway and a clear recommended next action.

Customization added for Scenario 04:
> For Signal, an analytics suite with a 60% bounce rate. The user is a PM or growth lead doing a Monday metrics review who can't tell what matters. Use synthetic B2B SaaS data. The most important insight this week: account churn rose 36%, almost entirely among Starter-plan accounts.

## What V1 shows
1. **Most important insight first:**
   - Headline metric: account churn rate **4.4%, ▲ +36%** vs a typical week (3.2%).
   - Plain-language takeaway: "More accounts are cancelling than usual, and almost all of the increase is coming from Starter-plan customers."
   - Recommended next action: "Ask Customer Success to contact at-risk Starter-plan accounts this week."
2. **Everything else (no unusual changes):** sign-ups, activation rate, trial-to-paid and new bookings, each marked "normal range."

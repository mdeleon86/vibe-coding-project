## Show & Swap read, round 2

_A NEW partner, a blind read. What landed differently from v1?_

- **Feels like a real product, or a mockup?** It feels like an early-stage analytics product. The churn warning, performance indicators, and recommended action create a realistic business workflow.
- **Where interactivity fell short:** I would expect the recommended action to let me identify or open the actual at-risk accounts. The dashboard tells me what to do, but doesn't let me carry out that action.
- **Would they show it to a VP?** Yes, as a prototype demonstrating how teams could identify and respond to churn. Not yet as a production-ready solution because the metrics are synthetic and the recommendations aren't connected to real customer data.
